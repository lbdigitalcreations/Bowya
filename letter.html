Chapter 01 • Personal Tribute

A Letter From The Heart

"Some people come into our lives unexpectedly and leave behind a bond we never knew we needed. You are one of those people for me." ❤️

💌

Dear Brother,

Some people enter our lives unexpectedly, but somehow become a part of our hearts before we even realise it. You are one of those people for me. When I first came into a new place, I could have easily felt alone and lost. But you never let me feel that way. You accompanied me, made me feel comfortable, stood by me and, without even trying, behaved more like a brother than just someone I happened to know. Maybe that is where this bond truly began. You have carried responsibilities from such a young age and fought battles that you rarely speak about. Yet, despite everything life has placed on your shoulders, you still choose to smile, enjoy the little things, explore life and find reasons to be happy. Watching you has taught me something beautiful — that having struggles doesn't mean we have to stop choosing happiness. Somewhere along the way, your way of living changed me too. I have learned to hold myself together a little better, to look for happiness even on difficult days, and to cry much less than I used to. You may not know it, but your strength has quietly become a lesson for me. There is another quality in you that I deeply admire — you don't speak badly about people behind their backs. Even when someone has hurt you, misunderstood you or doesn't like you, you still try to help when you can. That kind of goodness, especially when nobody is watching, says a lot about a person's character. And what makes it even more special to me is that you helped me even before you really knew me. You guided me during my negotiations, gave me advice when I needed it, and in ways you probably don't even realise, you contributed to some of the appreciation I received at my workplace. You have been carrying your own battles while still making space for other people's struggles. You carry responsibilities on your shoulders, kindness in your heart and somehow still manage to carry happiness with you. Maybe that's why I became so close to you. Not because of how long I have known you, but because of how you made me feel when I needed someone — like I wasn't alone, like I had a brother beside me. I genuinely hope life gives you the kind of happiness you so effortlessly try to give everyone around you. May every responsibility you carry become lighter, every dream you chase come closer, and every battle you've fought someday make sense. You may simply think you're living your life, but somewhere along the way, you became someone who taught me how to live mine a little more happily. And for that, I'll always be grateful. ❤️
Happy 25th birthday to someone who has become incredibly special to me. ❤️ It still amazes me how unexpectedly you came into my life and how, in such a short time, you became someone I can genuinely call my brother. You are not connected to me by blood, but the place you hold in my heart is no less than that of a real brother. You are someone I value deeply, and I want you to always remember how important you are to me. As you step into this beautiful new chapter of your life, I want you to know that I wish nothing but the very best for you. I hope you achieve everything you dream of, build the life you truly want, and become incredibly successful in everything you choose to do. Please don't worry too much about what life has in store for you. Things will fall into place in their own time. Keep believing in yourself and keep moving forward. ❤️ I know how much you love exploring, discovering new places and experiencing new things, and I promise you, there are still so many places waiting for us. From little adventures to crazy plans and unforgettable journeys, I want to be there to explore them with you. Whenever you feel like going somewhere, just tell me — we'll figure it out and make the memories happen. I genuinely look forward to all the places we are yet to see and all the stories we are yet to create. And whenever life feels difficult, please remember that you don't have to carry everything alone. Don't worry about anything. Take life one day at a time, trust yourself and keep going. I will always be someone who wishes the best for you, supports you and wants to see you happy. I love you loads, my dear brother. ❤️ You have become a very important person in my life, and I'm genuinely grateful that our paths crossed. Some people come into our lives unexpectedly and leave behind a bond we never knew we needed. You are one of those people for me. More than anything, I want to see you happy, successful, confident and living a life that makes you proud. I can't wait to watch you grow, achieve your dreams, travel, explore, and become everything you are capable of becoming. Here's to 25 years of you and to all the beautiful years that are still waiting for you. ✨ Keep smiling. Keep exploring. Keep dreaming big. And remember — you have a sister in me, always. ❤️

With lots and lots of love,

Your non-blood sister ❤️
