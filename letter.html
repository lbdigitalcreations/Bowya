Dear Brother,

Some people enter our lives unexpectedly, but somehow become a part of our hearts before we even realise it. You are one of those people for me.

Met You Recently

I met you very recently, and when I first came into a new place, I could have easily felt alone and lost. But you never let me feel that way. You accompanied me, made me feel comfortable, stood by me and, without even trying, behaved more like a brother than just someone I happened to know. Maybe that is where this bond truly began.
Met You Recently 🏫✨
When I First Came into a New Place
You have carried responsibilities from such a young age and fought battles that you rarely speak about. Yet, despite everything life has placed on your shoulders, you still choose to smile, enjoy the little things, explore life and find reasons to be happy. Watching you has taught me something beautiful — that having struggles doesn't mean we have to stop choosing happiness.
“Somewhere along the way, your way of living changed me too. I have learned to hold myself together a little better, to look for happiness even on difficult days, and to cry much less than I used to. You may not know it, but your strength has quietly become a lesson for me.” 💖

Quiet Strength & Character

There is another quality in you that I deeply admire — you don't speak badly about people behind their backs. Even when someone has hurt you, misunderstood you or doesn't like you, you still try to help when you can. That kind of goodness, especially when nobody is watching, says a lot about a person's character.

And what makes it even more special to me is that you helped me even before you really knew me. You guided me during my negotiations, gave me advice when I needed it, and in ways you probably don't even realise, you contributed to some of the appreciation I received at my workplace.
Quiet Strength ✨
Pure Heart & Character

He Keeps Everyone Happy

You have been carrying your own battles while still making space for other people's struggles. You carry responsibilities on your shoulders, kindness in your heart and somehow still manage to carry happiness with you.

He has a heart that chooses happiness, no matter what life puts him through. And wherever he goes with his friends, he leaves a little of that happiness behind. He keeps people happy who is around him.

Maybe that's why I became so close to you. Not because of how long I have known you, but because of how you made me feel when I needed someone — like I wasn't alone, like I had a brother beside me.
Keeps Everyone Happy 🌊✨
Friends & Joy Around Him
I genuinely hope life gives you the kind of happiness you so effortlessly try to give everyone around you. May every responsibility you carry become lighter, every dream you chase come closer, and every battle you've fought someday make sense.

You may simply think you're living your life, but somewhere along the way, you became someone who taught me how to live mine a little more happily. And for that, I'll always be grateful. ❤️

With lots and lots of love,

Your non-blood sister ❤️
