# Many candles to blow, Jihin Jino (Chamathu) | 25th Birthday Celebration 🎂✨

A personalized, luxury 25th birthday celebration website built for Jihin Jino (Chamathu) by his non-blood sister. Born on October 10, 2001, turning 25 on October 10, 2026!

## ✨ Features
- **Cinematic Entry & Live Countdown**: Unlocks into a cosmic birthday celebration with confetti bursts and audio.
- **25-Candle Milestone Cake**: Interactive blowout with sound effects, smoke physics, and celebration fanfare.
- **A Sister's Letter Envelope**: Interactive two-chapter parchment envelope with wax seal:
  - *Chapter 1: The Bond That Changed My Life* (Heart-to-heart gratitude letter)
  - *Chapter 2: 25th Milestone & Adventures* (25th milestone journey blessing)
- **The Secret Adventure Surprise**: Personalized explorer trip voucher and celebration message.
- **6 Spotlight Tributes & Memories Gallery**: High-resolution photo cards with deep quotes and full-screen lightbox preview:
  - *Just Getting Started* (Solo Portrait)
  - *The Free Spirit* (Bike Explorer)
  - *Where The Heart Belongs* (With Mother)
  - *The Joy In Every Room* (With Friends on Beach)
  - *The Bond Beyond Blood* (With Non-Blood Sister)
  - *The Quiet Strength & Pure Heart* (Milestone Formal in Purple Shirt)
- **Jihin's 25th Birthday Mini-Games Arcade**:
  1. **Memory Match**: 3 difficulties (Easy/Medium/Hard), countdown timer, move & match trackers, 1 free hint per round, and 3D card flips.
  2. **Whack-A-Present**: Fast-paced 30-sec reflex blitz, golden presents (+50), penalty bombs, combo streak multiplier up to 4x, and floating points.
  3. **This or That**: 12 randomized questions, interactive cards, back navigation, and end-game personality archetype report.
  4. **Birthday Mystery Escape (Mission 25)**: 3-stage flagship escape room featuring interactive celebration room clue search, milestone riddle solver, and tactile cipher keypad (2525) to unlock the grand birthday surprise!
- **Arcade Hall of Fame**: Persistent personal best tracking via LocalStorage.
- **Web Audio API Synthesizer**: Zero external audio dependencies, responsive sound effects (pops, chimes, buzzers, fanfare, keypad tones) and global mute toggle.
- **Theater Mode**: Accessible directly inside `index.html#arcade` or full-screen via `arcade.html`.
- **Instant WhatsApp Sharing & QR Code Scanner Modal**: Easily share and open on mobile devices.

## 🚀 Getting Started
Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser or deploy directly to GitHub Pages / Netlify / Vercel.
